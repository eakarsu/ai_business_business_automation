I've analyzed your system and found the complete workflow for viewing vendor products and creating bids. Here's your step-by-step guide:

  How to View Vendor Products and Create Bids

  1. Login to the System

  - Go to http://localhost:3000/login (or your frontend URL)
  - Use your registered credentials

  2. Navigate to Products

  From the dashboard, you have two ways to access products:
  - Option A: Click "Products" in the navigation menu
  - Option B: Use the direct URL http://localhost:3000/products

  3. Browse Vendor Products

  The products page (/products) shows:
  - Product catalog with vendor products in a grid layout
  - Filter options by:
    - Category (Technology, Office Supplies, Construction, etc.)
    - Vendor (dropdown showing all vendors)
  - Product details for each item:
    - Product name and description
    - Vendor name
    - Category
    - SKU (if available)
    - Unit price
    - Stock quantity
    - Lead time
    - Active/Inactive status
    - In Stock/Out of Stock status

  4. View Bids Page

  To access the bidding system:
  - Go to http://localhost:3000/bids
  - Or click "View Bids" from the dashboard

  5. Create a New Bid

  On the bids page:
  1. Click the "Add Bid" button
  2. Fill out the bid form:
    - Bid Title: Name your bid
    - Vendor Name: Enter vendor name
    - Amount: Enter bid amount in USD
    - Category: Select from dropdown (IT Services, Office Supplies, Manufacturing, Consulting, Other)
    - Description: Detailed description of the bid
  3. Click "Add Bid" to submit

  6. Track Your Bids

  The bids page shows:
  - All bids with filtering by status:
    - Submitted
    - Under Review
    - Approved
    - Rejected
  - Bid details including:
    - Title, vendor, amount, category
    - Description and submission date
    - Current status
  - Status management dropdown to update bid status

  7. Navigation Flow

  Your typical workflow:
  1. Dashboard → Quick overview of stats
  2. Products → Browse available vendor products
  3. Bids → Create bids for products you want to procure
  4. Dashboard → Monitor bid status and procurement metrics

  Key Features Available:

  - Product filtering by category and vendor
  - Real-time bid status tracking
  - Comprehensive product information including pricing and availability
  - Bid management with status updates
